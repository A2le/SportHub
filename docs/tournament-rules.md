# SportHub Tournament Rules & FAQs

## General Tournament Rules

### Registration
- **Team Size**: Each team must have a minimum of 11 players and a maximum of 18 players
- **Age Groups**: Teams must register in their correct age category (U11, U13, U15, U17, U19, Senior)
- **Registration Deadline**: Teams must register at least 7 days before tournament start
- **Registration Fee**: R500 per team (payable upon registration)
- **Late Registration**: Additional R200 fee if registered less than 7 days before event

### Team Composition
- **Players**: All players must be registered before the first match
- **Coach**: Each team must have a registered coach present at all matches
- **Substitutes**: Up to 5 substitutes allowed per match
- **Player Cards**: All players must have valid ID cards or birth certificates for age verification

### Match Rules
- **Match Duration**: 
  - U11, U13: 2 x 25 minutes
  - U15, U17: 2 x 30 minutes
  - U19, Senior: 2 x 40 minutes
- **Rest Between Matches**: Minimum 60 minutes rest required between matches
- **Kick-off Time**: Teams must be ready 15 minutes before scheduled kick-off
- **Late Arrival**: Teams arriving more than 10 minutes late forfeit the match (0-3)

### Equipment
- **Uniform**: All teams must wear matching jerseys with numbers
- **Shin Guards**: Mandatory for all players
- **Boots**: Metal studs not allowed on artificial turf
- **Ball**: Size 5 for U15 and above, Size 4 for U11 and U13

### Discipline
- **Yellow Card**: Warning - player continues
- **Red Card**: Player ejected, team plays with one less player
- **Accumulated Cards**: 2 yellow cards in tournament = 1 match suspension
- **Violent Conduct**: Immediate tournament ban and possible club sanctions

## Fixture Schedule

### How Fixtures Are Generated
- **Automatic**: Fixtures are automatically generated when tournament reaches capacity
- **Fair Play**: Algorithm ensures balanced schedule and adequate rest times
- **Notifications**: Teams receive fixtures via email and WhatsApp
- **Changes**: Fixture changes due to weather will be communicated 24 hours in advance

### Match Times
- **Start Time**: Typically 8:00 AM
- **End Time**: Usually by 5:00 PM
- **Break Times**: Lunch break from 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM

## Scoring & Rankings

### Points System
- **Win**: 3 points
- **Draw**: 1 point
- **Loss**: 0 points
- **Forfeit**: 0 points (opposing team gets 3 points and 3-0 score)

### Tie-Breakers (in order)
1. Total points
2. Goal difference
3. Goals scored
4. Head-to-head result
5. Penalty shootout (if championship deciding)

## Venue Information

### Pitch Facilities
- **Number of Pitches**: Varies by venue (typically 2-4)
- **Surface**: Grass or artificial turf
- **Changing Rooms**: Available for all teams
- **Parking**: Free parking available

### Spectators
- **Entry**: Free for spectators
- **Refreshments**: Food and drinks available at venue
- **Seating**: Limited seating - bring camping chairs recommended

## Safety & First Aid

### Medical Support
- **First Aid**: Qualified first aiders on site
- **Emergency**: Ambulance on standby
- **Insurance**: Teams must have their own sports insurance
- **Medical Forms**: Required for all players under 18

### Weather Policy
- **Rain**: Matches continue unless pitch unsafe
- **Lightning**: Immediate suspension - resume when safe
- **Extreme Heat**: Water breaks every 20 minutes

## FAQs

### Q: Can we register multiple teams from same school?
**A:** Yes! Schools can register multiple teams in different age groups.

### Q: What happens if we can't make a match time?
**A:** Contact tournament director immediately. Late cancellations may result in forfeit.

### Q: Can we change our registered players?
**A:** Yes, up to 24 hours before your first match. No changes after that.

### Q: What if it rains?
**A:** Matches continue unless pitch becomes unsafe. Waterproof gear recommended.

### Q: How do we get our fixture schedule?
**A:** Automatically sent via email and WhatsApp when fixtures are generated.

### Q: Can parents watch?
**A:** Absolutely! Spectators are welcome and entry is free.

### Q: What if a player gets injured?
**A:** First aid available on-site. Serious injuries - ambulance will be called.

### Q: Are medals/trophies awarded?
**A:** Yes! Winners and runners-up receive medals. Champion receives trophy.

### Q: Can we bring our own referees?
**A:** No, all referees are assigned by tournament organizers.

### Q: What's the protest procedure?
**A:** Protests must be submitted in writing within 30 minutes of match end with R500 deposit.

### Q: How do I register via WhatsApp?
**A:** Simply message "register" to +1 415 523 8886 and follow the prompts!

### Q: Can we register on the day?
**A:** Only if spots available and with late registration fee. Not recommended.

### Q: What payment methods accepted?
**A:** Bank transfer, cash, or card (on-site only).
